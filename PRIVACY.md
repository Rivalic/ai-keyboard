# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: December 6, 2025**

## Introduction

AI Keyboard ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our iOS keyboard application.

## TL;DR

**We don't collect, store, or transmit any of your data. Everything happens on your device.**

## Information Collection and Use

### What We DON'T Collect

AI Keyboard does NOT collect, store, or transmit:
- ❌ Typed text or keystrokes
- ❌ Personal information
- ❌ Usage statistics
- ❌ Analytics data
- ❌ Crash reports
- ❌ Device information
- ❌ Location data
- ❌ Any other user data

### What Happens on Your Device

All AI processing happens locally on your device:
- ✅ Text predictions are generated on-device
- ✅ Grammar checking runs locally
- ✅ Settings are stored locally
- ✅ No data leaves your device

## Data Storage

### Local Storage Only

The app uses:
- **UserDefaults**: To store your preferences (dark mode, accent color, feature toggles)
- **App Groups**: To share settings between the main app and keyboard extension

All data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

### What's Stored Locally

- Your appearance preferences (dark mode, accent color)
- Your feature preferences (AI features on/off)
- Your typing preferences (auto-capitalization, haptic feedback, etc.)

This data:
- Stays on your device
- Is not backed up to cloud services
- Is deleted when you uninstall the app

## Third-Party Services

AI Keyboard does NOT use any third-party services:
- ❌ No analytics services (Google Analytics, Firebase, etc.)
- ❌ No crash reporting (Crashlytics, Sentry, etc.)
- ❌ No advertising networks
- ❌ No cloud services
- ❌ No external APIs

## Full Access Permission

### Why We Request It

iOS keyboard extensions require "Allow Full Access" permission to:
- Access the Natural Language framework for AI predictions
- Provide haptic feedback
- Access certain iOS APIs

### What We Do With It

Even with full access, we:
- ✅ Process everything on-device
- ✅ Never transmit data
- ✅ Never store typed text
- ✅ Never access the network

### You Can Verify

AI Keyboard is open source. You can review the code to verify:
- No network requests are made
- No data is transmitted
- All processing is local

## Children's Privacy

AI Keyboard does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Since we don't collect any data at all, the app is safe for users of all ages.
